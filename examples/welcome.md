# Just a markdown editor

A little space to think, write, and keep your notes as plain Markdown.

Click a line to see its source. Move away and the markers disappear.

## Make yourself at home

Write with **bold**, *italic*, and ~~strikethrough~~. Add [a link](https://example.com), or keep a little `code` inline.

- [ ] Write your first note
- [ ] Click this checkbox
- [x] Keep everything in ordinary files

1. Open a workspace folder with Ctrl+O, or a file with Ctrl+Shift+L.
2. Save with Ctrl+S.
3. Undo with Ctrl+Z and redo with Ctrl+Y.

> Give an idea room to become a thought.

## A quiet corner

Press F11 for focus mode. Esc brings the window back.
